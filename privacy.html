PaisaCalc — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Summary

PaisaCalc does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or financial data. Everything you enter — salary, expenses, savings goals, or any other figures — stays only on your own device.

What data the app uses

  • Numbers you type into the calculator, budget, savings, and interest tools are used only to show you a result, right there on the screen.
  • Your budget, salary, and savings entries are saved using your browser/device's local storage so they're still there next time you open the app.
  • This data is never sent to us, to any server, or to any third party. We have no ability to see, access, or recover it.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with advertisers, analytics companies, or anyone else, because no data ever leaves your device in the first place.

Data deletion

You can clear all saved data at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this app, or by uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

PaisaCalc does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, since it collects no data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this same page with a new "Last updated" date.
